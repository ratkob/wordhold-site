Support

Something not working, or a question about the app? Write to us and a real person (the developer) will answer.

Email: wordhold@ratko.biz

In the app, Settings › Send feedback opens an email with your app version and iPhone model already filled in, which helps us fix problems faster.

We reply within 3 business days.

Common questions

How do I get my words in?

Any of these: select a word or passage in another app (for example Safari or the Kindle app) and tap Share, then Wordhold; photograph your e-reader's word list (Kindle Vocabulary Builder or Kobo My Words), photograph a page of a paper book and tap the words, type a word, say "Add a word to Wordhold" to Siri, or import a list: a CSV or text file from another app, or a list you paste in.

The camera missed a word or read one wrong

Words the app isn't sure about are shown under "To check" on the confirm screen before anything is saved. Fix or remove them there. Good light and holding the phone flat over the screen help most. If a list keeps failing, email us a description of your e-reader model; please don't send photos of anything private.

Are my words backed up? Will they move to a new iPhone?

Yes, if iCloud is on for Wordhold. Your words, books and review history sync through your own private iCloud storage, so they appear on a new iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account. You can also export everything at any time (below).

How do I export my words?

Go to Settings › Export your words, or tap Export on the Words screen. You can save a CSV file (opens in Numbers, Excel or Google Sheets) or a file for Anki. Exporting is always free.

Is Wordhold free?

Yes. Wordhold is free while it launches, with every feature and no limit on words. If you open it during this free launch, you keep everything free for good, including features added later, even if Wordhold starts selling subscriptions. This carries over to a new iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud on.

How do I delete my data?

See Your choices and rights in the privacy policy. In short: deleting the app removes the words on your phone; your iCloud copy can be deleted in your iPhone's iCloud settings.

Is Wordhold connected to Amazon or Kobo?

No. Wordhold is an independent app. It reads the word list you show it with your phone's camera; it never signs in to your Amazon or Kobo account.

Wordhold is an iPhone app that teaches you the words you looked up in your own books.
