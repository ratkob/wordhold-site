Sources and licences

Wordhold stands on the work of others. These are the data, code and fonts the app uses, and the licences they are shared under. The same list, with the full licence texts, is in the app under Settings › About › Sources.

Dictionary

Open English WordNet 2025

Definitions, word senses, synonyms and example sentences come from Open English WordNet (2025 edition), copyright © 2019–present, the Open English WordNet team, based on Princeton WordNet 3.1 (copyright © 2011 by Princeton University). Licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0). Source: github.com/globalwordnet/english-wordnet. Wordhold converts it to its own database format and shows only the senses and examples that fit each word; the wording of definitions is unchanged.

Princeton WordNet: George A. Miller (1995), WordNet: A Lexical Database for English, Communications of the ACM 38(11), 39–41. Princeton University, "About WordNet", 2010.

Word frequency

Google Books Ngram

How common each word is comes from the Google Books Ngram dataset (English 1-grams, version 20200217), counted from books printed since 1900. Licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported licence (CC BY 3.0). Wordhold keeps only the 100,000 most frequent words, folded to lowercase.

Scheduling

FSRS

Reviews are scheduled with the FSRS algorithm from the Open Spaced Repetition project. Wordhold uses its own Swift port of py-fsrs, tested against py-fsrs's results. py-fsrs is licensed under the MIT licence, Copyright (c) 2022 Open Spaced Repetition.

Example sentences

Project Gutenberg

Second example sentences are single sentences of 8 to 25 words chosen from about 1,500 public-domain books made available by Project Gutenberg. Each sentence names its author and book. Project Gutenberg's licence text and trademark are not part of the extracted sentences. See Project Gutenberg's permissions page.

Word roots

The word roots and affixes in the app were written for Wordhold.

Fonts

  • Literata: Copyright 2017 The Literata Project Authors. Licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
  • Atkinson Hyperlegible Next: Copyright 2020–2024 The Atkinson Hyperlegible Next Project Authors. Licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

Book covers

Book covers and authors, when shown, come from Open Library, a project of the Internet Archive, under the Open Library terms. They are the only things the app looks up online; everything else above comes inside the app.

Apple frameworks

Text recognition, on-device meaning picking, iCloud sync, widgets and speech use Apple's built-in iOS frameworks.
