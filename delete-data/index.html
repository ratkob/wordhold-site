Data deletion instructions

To have your Wordhold data deleted, send an email to wordhold@ratko.biz with the subject Delete my data. There is no form, no account and no login, because Wordhold has no accounts.

What to put in the email

We don't know who you are, so we need an ID to find your records. Open Wordhold, go to Settings › About and copy the Install ID, then paste it into your email. If you can't find it, write to us anyway and we will explain how to find it. If you never shared usage data, the services hold nothing tied to an ID for you, and we will tell you so.

What we can delete

  • Usage data, crash reports and ad measurement. PostHog, Google Firebase and Singular hold these under the random install ID, not your name. Send us the ID and we will have what is linked to it deleted. The privacy policy says what each one receives and how long it is kept.
  • Subscription records. RevenueCat holds these, once Wordhold sells subscriptions. The same request covers them. Purchases made while usage data was off are kept under an ID the app doesn't show; for those, include the order ID from Apple's purchase receipt email.
  • Your emails to us. Tell us if you want them deleted too; we otherwise keep them for two years.

What you delete yourself

Your words, books and practice history live on your own iPhone and in your own iCloud, where we can't read them. We can't delete them for you, but you can:

  • In the app: Settings › Delete all words deletes every word with its reviews, books and shelves; Settings › Start from scratch also deletes your habit, badges, practice history and settings. Both delete from your iPhone, the spare copy and your iCloud, so your other devices on the same Apple Account are emptied too.
  • On your iPhone: delete the Wordhold app. This removes your words and the app's spare copy of them. A couple of small markers (no words) stay in your iPhone's Keychain until the iPhone is erased; the privacy policy explains them.
  • In iCloud: open Settings, tap your name, then iCloud › Manage Account Storage (or Manage Storage), choose Wordhold and delete its data.

Apple keeps its own records of your purchases under your Apple Account; those are handled by Apple, under Apple's privacy policy.

How soon we reply

We will reply as soon as we can, and within the time the law asks. For the full list of your choices and rights, see Your choices and rights in the privacy policy.
