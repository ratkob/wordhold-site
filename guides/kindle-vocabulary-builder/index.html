How to find and use Kindle Vocabulary Builder

Every time you look up a word on a Kindle, it can save the word and the sentence you found it in. That list is called Vocabulary Builder. Here is how to find it and use it.

Written from Amazon's user guides for current Kindle e-readers (Kindle, Paperwhite, Colorsoft and Scribe). Older models and other software versions can name the menus slightly differently.

1. Check that it is switched on

Words you look up are added to Vocabulary Builder only while it is on, so check this first.

  1. Tap the top of the screen or swipe down from it to open Quick Actions, then tap All Settings.
  2. Tap Reading Options (on Kindle Scribe, Books and Notebook Options). On some models Vocabulary Builder sits one level further, under Language Learning.
  3. Make sure Vocabulary Builder is On.

2. Look up words as you read

  1. In any book, select a word to look it up. Cards with its meaning appear (dictionary, and sometimes Wikipedia or a translation); swipe between them.
  2. That's all. Every word you look up in the dictionary is added to Vocabulary Builder automatically, with an example of how it was used.

3. Open Vocabulary Builder

  1. From inside a book: tap the top of the screen to show the toolbar, tap the menu (⋮), then Vocabulary Builder.
  2. From the Home screen: open the menu and tap Vocabulary Builder.

4. Find your way around the list

  • Words (top left) shows every word you looked up. Tap a word to see its definition and the example from your book.
  • Books groups the words by the book you were reading. This is the easiest way to see the words from one book.
  • Learning shows how many words you are still learning and how many you have mastered.

5. Practise with Flashcards

  1. In Vocabulary Builder, tap Flashcards at the bottom of the screen. Kindle picks a set of words from the list you are viewing.
  2. Try to remember each meaning, then tap See Definition in the top left corner of the card.
  3. Tap Exit Flashcards to go back to the list.

What Vocabulary Builder doesn't do

The flashcards are a good start, but they do not decide when you should see each word again. Research on memory shows that words stick best when they come back just before you would forget them, and when you try to recall the meaning before you see it (see the science). Vocabulary Builder also only shows words from your Kindle, not from paper books or other e-readers.

Take your words into Wordhold

Wordhold reads the list straight from your Kindle's screen. Open Vocabulary Builder on the Words tab (or one book on the Books tab), point your iPhone at it in Wordhold, and the app reads the words with your phone's camera. The photo is processed on your phone and never uploaded. Wordhold then schedules each word for you, a few cards a day.
