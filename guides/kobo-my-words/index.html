How to switch on and use Kobo My Words

Kobo e-readers can keep a list of the words you look up, but the feature is hidden as a "beta feature" and is off until you switch it on. It's called My Words.

Written for Kobo e-readers with recent software. My Words arrived in a 2021 software update; if you don't see it, update your Kobo first (More › Settings › Device Information › Check for updates). Kobo says My Words isn't available on the Kobo Elipsa 2E. Menu names can differ slightly between models and versions.

1. Switch on My Words

  1. From the Home screen, tap More.
  2. Tap Beta Features.
  3. Find My Words and turn it on.

2. Save words as you read

  1. In any book, press and hold a word, then lift your finger. The dictionary box appears.
  2. Tap Add to My Words in the dictionary box.

3. Find your list

  1. Tap More › Activity, then the My Words tab.
  2. Use Sort to see the most recent words first or A to Z.

Your list is kept only on your Kobo, not in your Kobo account, so signing out or resetting the e-reader removes it. Kobo says an early version 6.0 software update could also clear it; versions 6.0.2 and later fix that.

What My Words doesn't do

My Words is a list, not a practice tool: it has no flashcards and no reminders. The list also doesn't show the sentence each word came from. Words stick best when they come back just before you would forget them, when you test yourself rather than re-read, and when you see them in context (see the science).

Take your words into Wordhold

Wordhold reads the My Words list straight from your Kobo's screen. Open the list, point your iPhone at it in Wordhold, and the app reads the words with your phone's camera. The photo is processed on your phone and never uploaded. If you want the sentence too, photograph the book page instead and tap the word.
